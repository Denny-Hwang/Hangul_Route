S2 A replay mark is not a replay button

How they differ: a mark has no fill, outline or label of its own, is 20 or 24 inside an answer that is 64 or more, and is hidden from screen readers; the answer around it is inactive, so a tap on it does nothing. Every action that shows replay (SoundNeededScreen.tsx:38) is a filled pill at least 64 high with a word beside the glyph. Nothing on a game screen pairs replay with "play again": Hear it uses speaker. The beta notes record how learners read it (spec 3.1).
