Method (gen2/analyze.mjs in the design pass): each probe is screenshotted in Chromium at 1x, 2x, 2.75x (Pixel 5) and 3x; at 1,440 angles the darkest device pixel across the stroke width is read and called ink when it is darker than the midpoint between the darkest sample and the paper next to the ring. Pass: a dotted ring shows exactly 8 dark runs with a dash-to-gap contrast of at least 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11), and a solid ring shows one unbroken run. Every render passes.
