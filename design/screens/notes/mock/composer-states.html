Wireframe design/wireframes/console/note-composer.md. A sub-view of the notes page: a modal dialog from 45em, a full-height view on a phone; Cancel, Close and Escape return to the list and focus to the opener. Presets are never ordered by progress; a report prefills the learner and maybe a quest, never a preset.
