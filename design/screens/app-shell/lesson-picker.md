# The grouped lesson picker on the console (screen spec, LV-3a)

Spec: owner report 2026-10-16 item 5 ("the lesson list is shown all at once, too many to take in"), `docs/specs/AMENDMENTS-LESSON-CHOOSER.md` (row **LV-3a**: the grouped chooser on the plan builder and the lecture page's catalogue; it amends F-PLAN-001 section 3.5 and `AMENDMENTS-LECTURE-V2.md` rows LV-3 and LV-4) · wireframe `design/wireframes/console/plan-builder.md` panels **G** and **H** (the builder's catalogue panel, which this replaces; the lecture page's Catalogue section takes the same picker in place of its flat list)
Built by: `apps/web/src/components/console/GroupedLessonPicker.tsx` over `chooserGroups` (`packages/content-schema/src/schemas/lesson-chooser.ts`); used by `teach/space/plan/page.tsx` (heading **Lessons**) and `LectureParts.tsx` `LectureCatalogueSection` (heading **Catalogue**)
Shots (Chromium, from `apps/web/e2e/lesson-picker.spec.ts` on the console e2e export): `shots/lesson-picker-plan-360.png`, `shots/lesson-picker-plan-1280.png`, `shots/lesson-picker-plan-320-text200.png`, `shots/lesson-picker-plan-uncovered-360.png` (a class no plan covers: Stage 1 and the wizard's line), `shots/lesson-picker-lecture-360.png`

## Why

The plan builder listed only Stage 1 (heading "Catalog (Stage 1)"), every episode with all its quests open; the lecture page listed the same Stage 1 block, then every story and topic, one long column (56 rows). Stages 2 to 7 could not be put in a plan from the builder at all, and titles were English in the es and ko console. The builder now lists the stages the space's learners can open (every ready stage for a space a Family Plan or a Group License covers, Stage 1 otherwise, spec section 4); the lecture catalogue lists every ready stage.

## Anatomy (top to bottom)

1. **h2**: the page's name for it (`console.chooser.planTitle` "Lessons" on the plan builder; `console.lecture.catalogue.title` "Catalogue" on the lecture page).
2. **Search** (`chooser.search.label`, a `type="search"` field at `touchTarget.min`) with its hint under it (`chooser.search.hint`, `aria-describedby`): matches the title in the console language, the Korean title in Hangul, its Revised Romanization, and a lesson's taught letters; accents folded, every word must match. While text is in it: **Clear** (`chooser.clearSearch.cta`), no chips, every matching group under its heading with its lessons shown, and a polite status line ("4 matches" / `chooser.noMatch`).
3. **Chips** (a native radio group, legend `chooser.groups` for screen readers only): Stage 1 to Stage 7 (only stages with a ready cell, and on the plan builder only those the space opens), then **Stories** and **Word topics** on the lecture page. One group shows at a time; the picker opens on the group of the first chosen item in the list's order, else the first group, and again whenever the page replaces its list (a stored draft, a link's add, another plan). A chip is `touchTarget.min` tall, `radii.pill`, `border.strong`; the chosen chip has a `borderWidth.thick` `text.primary` border (shape, not colour alone).
4. **Group heading** (h3): `chooser.heading.stage` "Stage 2 · 18 lessons", `.stories` "Stories · 24 stories", `.topics` "Word topics · 12 topics".
5. **Rows**, one per episode in theme order (five per stage): a checkbox row (title, then `chooser.episodeDetail` "3 lessons · about 13 min") that chooses the **whole episode**, and beside it a square button (`radii.md`, `touchTarget.min`, a CSS chevron of `borderWidth.thick` `text.primary` strokes that points right when closed and down when open) named `chooser.lessonsOf` "Lessons: {title}" with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` that shows the episode's lessons as indented checkbox rows ("about {n} min"). The whole-episode box is **mixed** (`indeterminate`, drawn as a dash) while some of its lessons are chosen. Stories and topics are single checkbox rows ("about {n} min").
6. **Full**: while nothing more fits (a classic plan holds 30 items, a route 260, a lecture outline 30 blocks), a box that would add more is off (`surface.sunken`) and the page's line shows on `feedback.nudgeLight` with a `feedback.nudge` edge (`chooser.full` on the plan builder, `lecture.outline.full` on the lecture page). A chosen lesson whose untick would add the other lessons of its whole episode beyond the room is off too; a box that removes is never off.
7. **Stages line** (plan builder only, spec section 4): while fewer stages than are ready are listed, the curriculum wizard's left-out line, word for word, sits under the chips in the same nudge style: `curriculum.preview.warnings.premium` with **See plans** for a class (free-plan switch off), `curriculum.freeDays.warningNoLessons` with **Open billing** (switch on, the owner of an uncovered space) or `curriculum.freeDays.warningNeutral` (switch on, anyone else). No price.

### The plan builder's sticky bar

Under the builder, a bar fixed to the bottom of the window while the page scrolls (`position: sticky`, `surface.canvas`, a `border.subtle` top line, bottom padding `max(spacing.sm, safe-area-inset-bottom)`): the count line (`chooser.chosen` "2 chosen · about 17 min", a status) above **Publish plan**, **Save draft** and Cancel. It is a labelled region (`chooser.bar` "Your choices"). Publish plan is in sight without scrolling at 360 x 640 and at 320 with 200 % text.

## Behaviour

- A press changes the page's own list **in place**: a plan item keeps its date, week and note, a lecture outline keeps its quizzes, activities, letters and class code where they are; a new item goes at the end.
- **A partly chosen episode's box**: on the lecture page the whole episode takes the place of its chosen lessons (one block); on the plan builder its missing lessons are added next to the chosen ones, which keep their dates and notes (a plan never merges).
- **Unticking one lesson of a whole episode** puts the other lessons at its place; on a plan each keeps the episode item's date, week and note. A lesson added beside a route lesson of its own episode takes that week and date.
- **Search** only narrows what is drawn: a row shows its matching lessons, and every press acts on the whole episode; the detail line is the whole episode's.
- Items the picker does not list (a story's listen quest a route added) stay in the plan untouched.
- Titles follow the console language (`catalogTitle`, the generated `catalogue-titles.generated.ts`), English where no overlay has a title.

## Accessibility

Every chip, box and button at least `touchTarget.min`; the focus ring is `borderWidth.thick` `border.focus` on the row label, the chip or the button. Text sizes are rem, so 200 % browser text doubles them; at 320 the chips and rows wrap and nothing scrolls sideways (`lesson-picker-plan-320-text200`). The lesson list of a closed episode is in the page with `hidden`, so `aria-controls` always names a real element. On the plan builder the page's `scroll-padding-bottom` is the measured height of the sticky bar, so Tab onto a row the bar covers scrolls it above the bar (WCAG 2.4.11). **Clear** keeps focus in the search field. The status line is a polite live region that stays in the accessibility tree while empty (visually hidden), so the first "n matches" is announced. The bar's count is rem; the console's shared buttons are px until the display rows give every console page a text size (spec section 9).
