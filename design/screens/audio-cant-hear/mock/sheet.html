The "Can't hear it?" sheet

Spec 3.3, 3.4, 3.7, 3.10. Built like VoiceHelpSheet: a bottom sheet (paper, radii.xl top corners, padding spacing.lg) over colors.surface.overlay. Title (a heading, first focus), Close at the end of the title row, exactly one line for this device, then Show the letters (primary) and Hear it (secondary, the speaker glyph). The scrim is not an accessibility element; the round behind is inert. Neutral: no Hoya, no amber, no red.
