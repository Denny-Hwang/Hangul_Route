Add a Korean voice

Menu names can differ on your device.

iPhone or iPad

1. Open Settings, then Accessibility.

2. Tap Spoken Content, then Voices, then Korean.

3. Download a voice, such as Yuna. Enhanced or Premium sounds best.

4. Come back and reopen Hangul Route.

You can still play: the sound is written on screen.

Add a Korean voice

Menu names can differ on your device.

Android

1. Open Settings, then search for Text-to-speech output.

2. Choose Speech Services by Google and tap the gear.

3. Tap Install voice data, then Korean.

4. Come back and reopen Hangul Route.

You can still play: the sound is written on screen.

Add a Korean voice

Menu names can differ on your device.

Computer

1. Mac: System Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, System Voice, Manage Voices, then Korean.

2. Windows: Settings, Time & language, Speech, Manage voices, then Add voices and Korean.

3. Chrome and Edge: some Korean voices need the internet, so check you are online.

4. Reload this page.

You can still play: the sound is written on screen.
