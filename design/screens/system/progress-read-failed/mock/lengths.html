Lengths at 320, Largest text

The longest cases in three languages at 320 wide with Largest text (1.5). Spanish and Korean are length drafts for this board only, not dictionary values: DP-1c and DP-1d carry the reviewed es and ko (R8 draft, F-I18N-001 3.10 review). Each screen frame is 320 x 568 and fixed: the body scrolls (to its end once the still-stuck line fills, D3) and the footer is pinned. Nothing may scroll sideways; every button stays at least 64 high; tools/capture.mjs checks that the footer ends inside the frame.
