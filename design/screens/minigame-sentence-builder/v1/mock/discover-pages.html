S2. pattern page

2 / 6

Look and learn

Here is a new way to say what something is.

Page 1 of 2

Sentence pattern

이에요/예요

___ ieyo / ___ yeyo

It is ___.

Use 이에요ieyo after a final consonant, 예요yeyo after a vowel.

Examples

책이에요.chaegieyoIt is a book.
학교예요.hakgyoyeyoIt is a school.
