Move Mina to another class

1. Ask the other teacher for their class code.

6 letters or numbers — no O, I, 0 or 1

2. Move to "Saturday Class B"?

  • The family is asked first. The other class sees nothing until the family says yes. Then it sees only initials, a picture and a level until the move.
  • Nothing changes until everyone has said yes, and then not for 2 days.
  • Mina keeps cards and stars. You can cancel any time before the move.
