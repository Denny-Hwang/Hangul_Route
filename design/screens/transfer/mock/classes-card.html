Profile, then Classes & family (SpacesCard). The variant is decided from the inbox and the device before any tap (classes-card.md, "Choosing the variant"), so no PIN is spent on a dead end. While a request is open, no row shows Move (one open request per learner); the pending row is the only action. Nothing new renders while transfersEnabled is false. A lock glyph marks a button behind the grown-up gate; its accessible name ends with "(PIN required)".
