Learn to read, hear and write Hangul, one five-minute quest at a time.

What you learn first

Stage 1 is the Korean alphabet: the letters, their sounds and how they build syllables.

  • Read letters and syllables
  • Listen and match sounds
  • Write letters by tracing them

After the alphabetWords, short sentences, conversations and heritage stories, in the same five-minute quests.

How most quests work

  1. 1Discover: see and hear a new letter.
  2. 2Play: short mini-games that use it.
  3. 3Check: a gentle round. Wrong answers get an amber nudge, never red.

Hoya the tiger guides every quest and never says “wrong.”

Questions

Is there a sign-in for learners?

No email or password needed. Open the app and choose your profile, or enter your class or family code. Moving to a new device? Use your Rescue Code.

I have a join code. Where do I enter it?

Open the join link your teacher shares. Or, in the app, tap Profile, go to Classes & family and tap Join a class.

Does it work offline?

On most devices, yes: after your first visit the app opens without a connection. Letter sounds may need a Korean voice installed on your device.

I’m an adult beginner. Will it feel too young?

No. Stage 1 teaches exactly what any beginner needs first — the letters and their sounds — with no baby talk. Hoya is friendly, and the culture cards are worth collecting at any age.

Do I need to know any Korean?

No. Starting from zero is what we built it for. Menus are in simple English, Spanish or Korean, and every Korean word comes with its romanization and meaning.

I already know some Hangul. Do I have to start at the beginning?

No. Play the first quest, add a name, then tap “Take the level check” on Home. Hoya suggests where to begin.
