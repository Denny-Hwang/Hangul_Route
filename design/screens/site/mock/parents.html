What learners do

Safe by design

  • No ads, and no data sold.
  • Learning data stays on the device until a grown-up signs in or the learner joins a class or family with a code. Then only a short progress summary is shared with that space.
  • A grown-up gate guards anything that costs money or deletes data.
  • Siblings can share a device. Each learner keeps their own stars and cards.
