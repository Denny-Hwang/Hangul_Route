Not now (GuestLater): the days without play, Add my name, Close (spec 8.6; wireframe guest-save.md Panel E)

The first line is the heading and takes focus. {days} is guestTtlDays(profiles): 30 on a device with no learner profile, 7 on a device that holds learner profiles. On an owner device the ask-the-owner line replaces Add my name and Close is the one filled button. Nothing is removed.
