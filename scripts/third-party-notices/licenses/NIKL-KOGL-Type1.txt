  국립국어원 (National Institute of Korean Language), 「한국어 학습용 어휘 목록」,
  published May 2003 (the page: "국립국어연구원에서 2003년 5월에 발표한 한국어 학습용 어휘 목록").

  work has been stated in full. The page reads: "공공저작물 자유이용허락 표시 기준
  (공공누리, KOGL) 제1 유형 조건에 따라 저작물의 출처를 구체적으로 표시한 후

  KOGL Type 1 is the attribution type: the work may be used, commercial use and
  adaptation included, as long as its source is stated.

Use in this repository:
  scripts/nikl-vocab-2003.json holds the list's 5,965 words (rank, word, homograph
  number, part of speech, gloss, grade A, B or C) converted to UTF-8 JSON by
  scripts/build-nikl-vocab.mjs, without any change to a word, a rank or a grade.
  It is development data: the placement lint (scripts/placement-rules.mjs) and the
  freq-coverage report of scripts/validate-content.mjs read it; no app bundle
  includes it. AMENDMENTS-PLC-VALIDITY §3.3.
