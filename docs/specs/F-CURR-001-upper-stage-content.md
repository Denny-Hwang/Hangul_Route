## 2. User story

> As **a learner of any age who finished Stage 1** (P4, P5 or P6), I want every cell of the grid I can see to open into real lessons that build on what I already read, so I go from words to sentences, to short conversations, to reading a menu or a train ticket, and finally to writing a few sentences of my own, in reading, listening and writing, with Hoya cheering me on.

> As **a parent or teacher**, I want each later cell to say plainly what it teaches, to be checked for language and culture before learners see it, and to fit the weekly plan I already made, so I can trust the content and plan around it.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

### 3.1 The rules every upper-stage cell follows

1. **Six steps per quest.** `intro -> discover -> practice -> apply -> check -> reward` (F-QUEST-002 R4). Discover is unscored and never skipped; Check is exactly 2 rounds scored on the first try, launcher "Two quick ones", no Skip; the reward step shows stars and the next-quest teaser.
2. **Timing (content-skill section 3.1, amended).** Per quest about 4-5 minutes (Stage 6 and 7 up to 5.5); `estimatedMinutes = round(sum(durationSeconds) / 60)` within 1 (F-QUEST-002 T10). Schema limits stay 3-7 steps and 3-15 minutes. The step ranges below are authoring ranges; the rule a test checks (U-5) is the **summed total** of a quest's six `durationSeconds`, which must fall in its stage's band: **Stage 2, 3 and 4: 225-300 s; Stage 6 and 7: 240-330 s**. Not every combination of step values inside the ranges lands in the band (all minimums or all maximums can fall outside), so an author picks step values whose sum does.

   | Step | Kind | Stage 2 | Stage 3 | Stage 4 | Stage 6 | Stage 7 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Hook | `intro` | 6-8 s | 6-8 s | 6-8 s | 6-8 s | 6-8 s |
   | Discover | `discover` | 40-60 s, 2 pages x 2 words | 50-75 s, pattern page + example page | 50-75 s, 2 exchange pages | 50-75 s, realia page + key words | 50-75 s, frame page + model page |
   | Play 1 | `practice` | 75-90 s, 6 rounds | 75-100 s, 5 rounds | 75-100 s, 3-4 turns | 90-110 s, 4-5 questions | 75-100 s, 4 rounds |
   | Play 2 | `apply` | 60-75 s, 4 rounds | 60-90 s, 4 rounds | 60-90 s, 4 rounds | 60-90 s, 3-4 rounds | 75-100 s, 3-4 rounds |
   | Check | `check` | about 35 s, 2 rounds | 35-45 s, 2 | 35-45 s, 2 | 35-45 s, 2 | 35-45 s, 2 |
   | Celebrate | `reward` | about 8 s | about 8 s | about 8 s | about 8 s | about 8 s |

3. **Quests per cell.** Three quests (about 13-15 minutes a cell), except Word Builder, which has six because it finishes the Hangul inventory (section 3.3). OD-2.
4. **Teach once, ask many** (R1 extended). Every new word, pattern or symbol is introduced in exactly one Discover step and then asked in that quest's games and Check and reused in later quests. New items per quest: Stage 2 at most 4 new words (Word Builder: at most 5 symbols of one family, or one new final sound for any number of final letters, section 3.3); Stage 3 one new pattern and at most 4 new words; Stage 4 one new grammar point and at most 4 new words or set phrases; Stage 6 at most 6 new words (realia carry many words, most of them known); Stage 7 one new frame and at most 3 new words. **Counting rules for U-3**: a run of digit names taught together on one Discover page (for example 일, 이, 삼, 사, 오) counts as **one** number-set item; a compound built only from items of the same quest (오천, ocheon, from 오 and 천) is not a new item. **Across cells of one stage**, rule 4 applies per cell: because a plan may order a stage's themes freely, an item that more than one cell of the same stage uses (for example 뭐, mwo, or 어디, eodi, in Stage 4) is Discovered in each of those cells, so it is never asked before it is shown on any route order; U-3 checks the caps within a cell, and U-6 counts an item as reused only when an earlier stage, Stage 1 or the same cell taught it.
5. **Reuse.** At least half of the Korean words a quest shows outside Discover were taught earlier (Stage 1, an earlier cell of the route order, or a vocabulary topic of the same level), the content-skill section 5 rule, now checked by a test (section 5, U-6).
6. **Decodable scored reading.** A scored round that requires reading Korean uses only symbols the route has taught by then: Stage 1's 30 symbols, plus, from Word Builder on, the symbols Word Builder has taught. Words with symbols not yet taught appear only as **theme words** (exposure in Discover, picture rounds and listen rounds, always with romanization and gloss), the F-QUEST-002 R3 rule carried up. Because a plan may order themes by motivation, the other Stage 2 cells never put a Word Builder symbol in a scored read round; they hear it or see it pictured.
7. **Every taught Korean string** (word, sentence, line, frame, realia text) carries `romanization` (Revised Romanization of the pronunciation, F-CNT-002: no hyphens, lower case except names, one romanized word per Korean word) and a gloss in the UI language: `en` in the cell file, `es` and `ko` in the overlays (section 3.10). The romanization is compared with the converter (`checkRomanization`); a mismatch fails CI unless an entry of `ROMANIZATION_EXCEPTIONS` covers it.
8. **Reading, listening and writing only.** No step records, measures or asks for speech; `voice-echo` is not allowed in a lesson cell (validator rule `lesson-no-speaking`). Lines a learner "says" in a dialogue are chosen by reading or listening (Tap to Respond); they are never spoken into the device. Writing is tile building, typing on an in-app Hangul keyboard and tracing (section 3.7).
9. **Anti-shame** (CLAUDE.md section 1). Wrong answers get the amber nudge and Hoya's `thinking` pose, never red; meters only fill; nothing a learner earned is taken away; no per-question timer; personal-choice rounds in Stage 7 have no wrong answer (section 3.7). Hoya's lines follow the Hoya voice of `design/brief/character/hoya-character-sheet.md` and F-HOYA-001 (the `content/characters/hoya/voice.md` path the content-skill names does not exist; this spec does not create it): warm, short, positive ("You read a real menu!"), never "failed", "wrong", "try harder".
10. **Audience any age.** No age range, no "for kids", no nursery tone. Scenes work for a child and an adult: Stage 7 "My Day" offers both 학교 (hakgyo, school) and 회사 (hoesa, work); Stage 4 "Playdate Talk" is friends playing a game, which reads naturally at any age.
11. **Culture claims are sourced** (section 3.9), and the cell carries both review sign-offs before it ships (section 3.8).
12. **No product prices.** Korean money amounts in Stage 6 (천 원, cheon won, one thousand won) are taught Korean, written as round practice amounts in a drawn scene; no lesson names or implies the price of any plan of this app, and no lesson names a real shop, brand or company.

### 3.3 Stage 2 — Words (three new cells)

Each new Stage 2 cell: practice is Pic-Word Match (6 rounds; WordArt or card pictures), apply is Build a Letter (4 rounds, each word built syllable by syllable) or Card Match, Check is Listen & Pick (a picture round, then a reading round on a decodable word). Pictures come from existing `word:` WordArt keys and `card:` art; a word with no picture is taught only as a reading word (never a picture round with a text tile, F-QUEST-002 section 3.11).

**Word Builder (`stage2-letters`, 6 quests, letters theme).** The letters column finishes the Hangul inventory that Stage 1 left open: the five tense consonants, the eleven compound vowels and the seven final sounds. A family of symbols that follows **one rule** (a doubled known consonant; two known vowels joined) may hold up to 5 symbols in one quest, which replaces R2's cap of 2 + 2 here: the learner meets one rule, not five shapes.

| Quest | Teaches (symbol keys) | Example reading words (romanization, gloss) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| q1 Vowel pairs ae and e | ㅐ ㅔ ㅒ ㅖ | 개 (gae, dog), 게 (ge, crab), 노래 (norae, song), 시계 (sigye, clock) | Many speakers today say ㅐ and ㅔ the same way; the Discover hint says so and the games never ask the learner to tell them apart by sound (culture-and-language claim, sourced: section 3.9) |
| q2 Double consonants | ㄲ ㄸ ㅃ ㅆ ㅉ | 까치 (kkachi, magpie), 딸기 (ttalgi, strawberry), 빵 (ppang, bread), 쌀 (ssal, uncooked rice) | One rule: a doubled letter is said tight, with no puff of air |
| q3 Joined vowels wa, wo, wi, ui | ㅘ ㅝ ㅟ ㅢ | 사과 (sagwa, apple), 과자 (gwaja, snack), 귀 (gwi, ear), 의자 (uija, chair) | Each joins two vowels the learner knows (ㅗ+ㅏ, ㅜ+ㅓ, ㅜ+ㅣ, ㅡ+ㅣ) |

### 3.4 Stage 3 — Sentences (five cells)

Practice is the **Sentence Builder** (`order-it`, row G-1): word tiles (어절, eojeol) tapped into order, and in some rounds a particle choice (이 i or 가 ga, 을 eul or 를 reul). Apply is Listen & Pick or Pic-Word Match on whole sentences (hear or read a sentence, pick the picture). Check is Listen & Pick: one heard sentence -> picture, one read sentence -> picture. Every sentence is in the polite 해요체 and ends with its sentence mark. A Discover pattern page shows the frame with its slot, two examples and a one-line UI-language note. The note is a cell field (`noteEn` in the cell file, `es` and `ko` in the lesson overlay, section 3.10), not a `packages/i18n` key, and any Korean inside it is an inline token like the story `{kw:}` tokens (for example `Use {ko:이} after a final consonant, {ko:가} after a vowel.`), so the rule "no Hangul in an `*En` field" (section 5) still holds and the token renders through `KoreanText` with its romanization.

### 3.5 Stage 4 — Dialogue (four new cells)

Practice is Tap to Respond (`tap-respond`, shipped): 3-4 turns, a heard and written line from the other speaker, three written replies. Distractor replies are never also natural replies (the F-PLC-001 `incompatibleWith` rule, checked by U-8). Every exchange is between named characters (Hoya and friends, a family at home), never the learner's own voice.

**내 / 네 in Playdate Talk.** 내 (nae, my) and 네 (ne, your) sound the same for most speakers today (the ㅐ / ㅔ merger of Word Builder q1), and everyday speech often says 니 (ni) for "your" to tell them apart. So volume 1 does not teach 네 차례야: q2 pairs 내 차례야 with 이제 너야, which sound different. No listen round, Check round or distractor may depend on telling 내 from 네 by ear, and U-8's `incompatibleWith` check lists the pair (내 차례야, 네 차례야) as one that must never be the two options of a heard round.

"Have you eaten?" as an everyday greeting (Kitchen Talk q1) and casual speech between friends (Playdate Talk) are culture-and-language claims with sources (section 3.9). Casual lines are only heard and read; the replies a learner picks model the polite 해요체 except in Playdate Talk, where a Discover page explains that friends of the same age use casual speech with each other, in one sentence, with no age range for the learner (OD-8). _Amended 2026-10-14 (S4-4 review): Playdate Talk says "close friends often use casual speech", the wording its two sources support (Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, 경어; Standard Korean Language Dictionary, 반말 「1」), with no age wording._

### 3.7 Stage 7 — Self-expression: the writing approach (five cells)

Stage 7 builds **writing without speaking**: the learner composes Korean on screen in three ways, from most to least supported.

1. **Build** — Sentence Builder tiles (G-1), as in Stage 3, now for 2-3 sentence pieces.
2. **Type** — **Type Hangul** (`type-hangul`, row G-3): an in-app two-set (dubeolsik) Hangul keyboard, the standard Korean layout (KS X 5002), in a fixed place under the prompt; the pure composition logic (jamo -> syllable blocks, final-consonant hand-over to the next syllable, backspace by jamo) lives in `packages/content-schema` (100 % covered). The system keyboard is not used (OD-10): the in-app keyboard is the same on every device and needs no Korean input method. A typed answer is checked against its target after the learner taps Done, by syllable, and a near miss shows the amber nudge with the target under it; a round is never marked wrong while the learner is typing.
3. **Trace** — **Trace a Word** (`trace-word`, row G-4): tracing a whole syllable block or a two-syllable word over a ghost, each jamo in its place in the block, with the stroke-order checks of F-005 / F-006 at the relaxed tolerance; the accessible path is the same as Trace Stroke's.

**Write Frame** (`write-frame`, row G-5) is the "my own sentence" round: a sentence frame with one or two slots (저는 ___을/를 좋아해요, jeoneun ___eul/reul joahaeyo, I like ___). The learner picks a word for each slot from a word bank of taught words (any pick is right: there is no wrong answer to "what do you like?"), then types or traces the picked word. The typing or tracing part is checked against the word the learner picked. **Allomorphs follow the pick**: the frame shows the particle or ending that fits the picked word's last syllable (이에요 after a final consonant, 예요 after a vowel; likewise 을/를, 은/는, 이/가), computed by a pure helper in `packages/content-schema` (row C-1) from the batchim; every frame writes its particle slot explicitly (`___을/를`, `___이에요/예요`, `___에`), and the helper fills each slot (에 has one form), so the finished sentence is always correct Korean; the learner types the word, and the helper's ending is shown already in place. The finished page is shown at the end of the Write Frame step, in the reward look, as "My page" (the quest's reward step itself is unchanged; design row D-3) and is **not kept** after the quest (OD-6): word-bank frames only, nothing stored, synced, sent or shown to anyone else, so no personal data is collected. A later spec may add a kept, local-only keepsake page after a privacy review.

**The one free-entry round: Write Your Name q3.** It is the only round in which the learner composes text that is not from a word bank (their own name), so it follows a data rule that row G-5 implements and S7-1 tests (test P-1, section 5):

- The composed text lives **only in the round component's state** (React state of the renderer). It is never written to a zustand store, a `persist` slice, the quest-state snapshot or any storage key, and it is cleared on unmount.
- It is **excluded from every telemetry payload** (`minigame.finished`, `quest.*` carry only ids, counts and codes), from crash and error breadcrumbs, and from logs.
- "My page", shown at the end of the Write Frame step, renders it from the same component state; it is not cached, not exported, has no share button, and the page is not part of any screenshot or thumbnail the app saves. The grown-up preview (F-BROWSE-001) shows the round with the cast name 호야 (hoya) prefilled, never a learner's entry.
- e2e and design screenshots use the cast name 호야 only, never a real name.
- The round is unscored, so nothing about it reaches progress except the quest completion.

### 3.9 Cultural topics and source requirements

- **What needs a source**: every statement of fact about Korean culture, history or custom (a Hoya line, a Discover note, a realia caption), and every language note that is not a plain gloss (for example "ㅐ and ㅔ sound alike for many speakers", "friends of the same age use casual speech" (shipped in S4-4 as "close friends often use casual speech", amended 2026-10-14), "여기요 is how you call staff"). A plain gloss of a word needs no source beyond the standard dictionary check of pass 4.
- **Bar**: at least **two sources**, at least one of them a Korean public institution or a reference it publishes: the National Folk Museum of Korea (Encyclopedia of Korean Folk Culture), the Academy of Korean Studies (Encyclopedia of Korean Culture), the Korea Heritage Service, Korea.net, the Korea Tourism Organization, the Korea National Park Service, or the National Institute of Korean Language (the Standard Korean Language Dictionary, the Korean orthography, the loanword spelling rules, the standard pronunciation rules). On source count this is stricter than story facts (F-STORY-001 section 3.6 asks for one authoritative source, two only for a numeric claim); on every other point it is the **same bar** as stories, the owner's heritage bar of exact citations fact-checked at least three times:
- **Source shape**: each entry of `sources[]` is the story `SourceSchema` (`packages/content-schema/src/schemas/story.ts`), reused, not copied: `{ title, publisher, url, accessed, quoteOrLocator }`, an `https` URL whose host is in `SOURCE_HOST_ALLOWLIST` (`story-sources.ts`; adding a host is a reviewed one-line PR), and an `accessed` date not in the future.
- **Three verification passes**: every culture note and every sourced language note carries a `verification` record in the story `VerificationSchema` shape (`passes`, `checkedAt`, `notes`; row C-1 adds `'lesson'` to its `scope` enum, an additive change) with `passes >= MIN_VERIFICATION_PASSES` (3): pass 2 (the author's sourcing), pass 2b (an independent fact-check session) and pass 5 (the MAR culture review, which re-checks every claim against its sources and is the final independent pass). A `ready` cell with fewer than three passes on any note fails `lesson-verification-passes`.
- **Source lints**, the story lints applied to lesson notes: `lesson-source-shape` (the shape and URL rules above), `lesson-copies-source` (a note shares no run of words with a source's `quoteOrLocator`, as `narration-copies-source`), `lesson-national-treasure-number` (no National Treasure or heritage designation number in a note, as `narration-national-treasure-number`).
- **Reuse**: a note that restates a fact already cited by a reviewed story may cite it as `factRef: "story:<slug>#<factId>"` instead of new sources (the Stage 5 book rule: no new fact, no new source); the story's own sources and three verification passes stand for it. The validator resolves every `factRef`.
- **Per cell** (minimum topics; the culture review may add or remove a note but not ship an unsourced one): Word Builder: the ㅐ / ㅔ note, the tense-consonant note (language notes). Holiday Words: Seollal and sebae; Chuseok and songpyeon; yut, kites and lucky pouches. Playground Words: jegichagi and spinning tops as traditional games. Stage 3 cells: no new culture claim beyond Stage 2's, except Festival Sentences (bowing and the full moon at Chuseok). Kitchen Talk: "have you eaten?" as a greeting. Playdate Talk: casual speech between friends. Read the Menu and Order Food: the won; 여기요; 잘 먹겠습니다. Travel for Chuseok: hometown travel at Chuseok. Hike & Ask: hiking and national parks. Buy at Market: traditional markets, the five-day market, 덤. Write Your Name: the loanword spelling rules (as a language note, never a promise that a name has one right spelling). My Holiday Memory, My Favorite Animal, Make & Share: reused facts and Stage 2 notes.
- **Never**: claims about what "all Koreans" do; national or ethnic stereotypes; religious practice presented as universal; a real living person; a real shop, brand, logo or company.
- **Link checks**: lesson source URLs join the monthly `check-source-links.mjs` run (row C-2).

```ts
  quests: z.array(LessonQuestSchema).min(3).max(6),          // QuestSchema (incl. optional rewardCardId) + scopes inline
  culture: z.array(CultureNoteSchema),                        // { id, textEn, sources: SourceSchema[] | factRef, verification: VerificationSchema }

});
// LessonQuestSchema = QuestSchema fields (id, title, steps, estimatedMinutes, teaches, rewardCardId?) + scopes[]
```

- Quest ids stay `quest:stage<n>-<theme>-q<k>`, episode ids `episode:stage<n>-<theme>` (the placeholder ids, so plans and homework that already name a placeholder episode keep working, F-PLAN-002 "New content"); scope refs `minigame:s<n>-<theme>-q<k>-<step>`.
- **Discover item types** (additive to `DiscoverItemSchema`, row C-1): `sentence` (a `KoText` with an optional picture), `pattern` (a frame with one slot marker, a note key and two example sentences), `exchange` (two lines, speaker A and B), `realia` (a scene-art ref and up to 6 key words). The existing `jamo` and `word` types are unchanged.
- **Minigame kinds** (additive to `MinigameKindSchema`): `realia-read`, `type-hangul`, `trace-word`, `write-frame`; Sentence Builder uses the existing `order-it`. Each gets a round schema next to the others in `schemas/minigame.ts`.
- **Glosses.** `en` lives in the cell file; `es` and `ko` in `content/i18n/{es,ko}/lessons-stage<n>-<theme>.json` (domain `lessons`), keyed by quest, scope and round ids like `minigames.json`, checked by the shared overlay rules (`overlay-rules.mjs`, allow-list in `overlay-fields.mjs`, banned words). Like vocabulary (`build-vocab.mjs`), the lesson glosses are compiled into the lesson module by `build-lessons.mjs`, not into `OVERLAYS`, so the package barrel does not grow (F-I18N-001 open item 2). Hoya lines, step titles and quest titles get their es and ko values in the same overlay file, so a cell ships complete in three UI languages.
- **Build.** `scripts/build-lessons.mjs` (row C-2) validates every cell, drops `draft` cells, and writes `apps/mobile/src/content/__generated__/lessons-stage<n>.ts` (one module per stage) plus `lessons-index.ts` (episode and quest headers: ids, titles, minutes, `teaches`, step list without scopes). `--check` fails on drift; `--report` prints raw and gzip bytes per cell and per stage and fails over budget (section 3.12). `content/episodes.ts` adds the generated episodes to `handWrittenEpisodes`, so `previewEpisodesFor` drops each cell's placeholder with no list to edit (F-STORY-005 section 3.5); `quests.ts` and `scopeFor` read the generated quests and scopes.
- **Sources stay out of the bundle.** `culture[].sources` are read by the validator and the link checker only; the app shows a culture note's text, not its sources (a Sources screen for lessons is out of scope).
- **The shipped taste quests** stay where they are (`quests.ts`, `minigame-config.ts`) until row T-1 / T-2 moves them (OD-3).

### 3.12 Catalogue, `CATALOG_VERSION` and the bundle budget

- **Catalogue.** Each cell row replaces its `soon(...)` with `cell(stage, theme, title, 'shipped', minutes, questIds)` and appends its `lesson(...)` rows with `teaches` (Word Builder: its symbol keys; other cells: `[]`, since they teach no symbol), bumps `CATALOG_VERSION` by 1 with a one-line note in the doc comment, adds the `LIVE_ROUTE_DAYS` and `LIVE_FREE_FACTS` entries for the new version in `packages/content-schema/src/__tests__/route-days.test.ts` (that test fails on a version with no entry), and keeps the parity test green. Rows that merge in parallel each bump from the version on `main` at rebase time (a rebase conflict on this line is resolved by taking the higher number plus one), and **move their pinned `LIVE_ROUTE_DAYS` / `LIVE_FREE_FACTS` entries to the new number** and recompute them on top of the cells that merged first. A shipped cell gives the Learning Route a new day of its minutes (F-PLAN-002 route days) and the wizard's **Refresh route** for older plans; plans that named the placeholder episode expand to its quests at derivation.
- **Stage state.** When the last placeholder of a stage ships, the stage reads "open" instead of "taste" (`stageAvailability`); the Learning Route's "Coming later" caption for that stage disappears by itself. Stage 3, 6 and 7 read "taste" from their first shipped cell.
- **Bundle.** The lesson modules are **separate from `stories.generated.ts`**, so the story bundle's last 9 KB gzip is not touched. Estimate from what ships: the 18 shipped lesson quests take about 65 KB raw / 13.5 KB gzip in `quests.ts` + `minigame-config.ts` (about 3.6 KB raw / 0.75 KB gzip each); upper-stage quests carry sentences, dialogues and two more gloss languages, estimated at 6-7 KB raw / 1.3-1.6 KB gzip each, so the 69 new-cell quests plus the 9 refreshed taste quests (T-1, T-2), 78 in the modules, come to about 510 KB raw / about 113 KB gzip, inside the total budget below. **Budget** (enforced by `build-lessons.mjs --report`): at most **7.5 KB raw per quest on average in a cell** (45 KB for Word Builder's six quests, 22.5 KB for a three-quest cell), **150 KB raw and 32 KB gzip per stage module**, **600 KB raw and 128 KB gzip in total**. The headers module (`lessons-index.ts`) is imported at start; the per-stage modules are reached through a lazy `require` inside `scopeFor` and the quest player, written as a `switch` on the stage with **one literal `require('./__generated__/lessons-stage2')` (and so on) per stage**, never a computed path (Metro and Hermes resolve only literal `require` strings; a template path fails the native build or pulls in the whole folder). A test asserts that `lessons-index.ts` imports no `lessons-stage*` module (the point of the split), and row C-2 checks the split with an iOS release build (`xcodebuild`) and a web export. So a stage's scopes are evaluated only when one of its quests opens (the F-STORY-001 "per-story lazy require" exit, applied from day one). On the web PWA, Metro still ships one bundle, so the download and the offline precache grow by the total (about 100 KB gzip, about 5 % of today's bundle); the lazy split saves evaluation time at start, which matters on the native build (F-STORY-001 PR 1.10). Raising a ceiling is a reviewed one-line change with the measured numbers in the PR.
- **Presenter font.** The lecture presenter font subset lists the Korean of lesson scopes (F-TCH-004 section 3.6, `build-presenter-font.mjs`). Each cell row rebuilds the subset and its glyph list; the 150,000-byte ceiling has room (the font is 40,820 bytes today).
- **Cards.** The 22 new cells add **no heritage cards** (OD-7, the Stage 5 book precedent): `rewardCardIds: []`, no `rewardCardId` on their quests. **The refreshed taste cells keep their shipped awards exactly** (T-1, T-2): `episode:stage2-life` keeps `rewardCardIds ['card:sagwa', 'card:uyu', 'card:ppang', 'card:dosirak']`, `episode:stage2-nature` keeps `['card:haneul', 'card:kkot', 'card:namu', 'card:byeol']`, `episode:stage4-rites` keeps `['card:annyeong', 'card:gamsa', 'card:chingu', 'card:insa']`, and `quest:stage2-life-q1`, `quest:stage2-nature-q1` and `quest:stage4-rites-q1` keep `rewardCardId` `card:dosirak`, `card:byeol` and `card:insa`; the new q2 and q3 of those cells award no card. The "no new cards" rule applies only to the 22 new cells. A learner's reward is stars, the Learning Route pips and the stage state; card art for Stages 2-7 is F-CARD-S2-001 .. F-CARD-S7-001, which become startable once their stage's cells ship. `card-award-integrity.test.ts` (T-AWARD-1..7) stays green because no new card exists. No `stage<N>-complete` card is added for Stages 2-7.

### 3.13 Free and paid access

- Every quest of a new cell is a **grid lesson** that is not free: `questAccess` returns `closed` for a free learner when the free-plan switch is on, `open-premium` for a subscriber, `open-switch-off` while the switch is off (today), and `open-kept` for a quest already finished (F-ENT-003 section 3.3). Stage 1 stays the whole free plan; the "free lessons" figures and `free-lessons-line.ts` do not change.
- With the switch on, a free learner sees each new cell as "Not open yet" with the dotted ring and the episode page's preview (F-ENT-003 section 3.11, wireframe `episode/detail-access-preview.md`); no learner screen names a product or a price.
- Grown-up preview (F-BROWSE-001): a paying parent or teacher sees every new cell exactly as a learner does; the free sampler shows Stage 1 only. Nothing is saved.
- Placement: a learner placed at `stage3` band 0 has every Stage 2 quest placed out (still playable, never marked done); no level places out Stage 3 or later (F-PLC-001 section 3.6 rule 1, unchanged).
- **New quests and stored placement results.** A stored placement result carries the exact `placedOutQuestIds` fixed when the check was taken (`appRouteCatalog` in `apps/mobile/src/logic/placement/route-skip.ts` does not recompute the level map, and `packages/content-schema/src/curriculum/generate.ts` uses the stored set when present and starts the route at the lowest stage with a ready quest outside it). Without a fix, every learner placed at `stage3` before S2-1 would find Word Builder q1 as their next quest once it ships. So row **C-2** (before any cell row) adds the **catalogue-growth rule**: when the effective route is computed (`effectiveRoute` / `placedOutSkipSet` in the app and the `generate.ts` queues), the stored set is widened with every quest that `questsPlacedOutAtLevel(result.level)` places out **and that was not in the catalogue when the result was stored**. To know that, a stored result gains an optional `catalogVersion` (additive; a result without it counts as version 8, the version in force before this rule), and the catalogue keeps the frozen quest-id list per version it needs for the comparison. Quests a grown-up override put back stay put back (they existed at storage time, so the rule never adds them), and quests the learner completed are never added. Regression test (C-2): a stored `stage3` result and a plan made at catalogue version 8, then a catalogue with Word Builder shipped; the next quest is still the first Stage 3 quest, and `startRaised` / `startPutBack` show no plan-update prompt.

### 3.14 Accessibility, audio, offline, privacy

- Taught Korean renders through `KoreanText` (romanization `always` or `tap`, F-I18N-001); a screen reader reads the gloss and the spaced romanization (`a11yRomanization`).
- Every listen prompt plays through `playPrompt` with the visible text fallback when no Korean voice is available (F-AUDIO-004); a round never depends on hearing alone without that fallback. Read Along's exchange Discover pages highlight each line as it plays and work with sound off (the lines stay readable).
- Type Hangul: every key has an accessible name (the jamo and its sound value, `JAMO_SOUND_VALUES`), the keyboard meets `touchTarget`, and the composed text is announced after each syllable. Trace a Word follows Trace Stroke's accessible path.
- Realia: every drawn text is also real text in the accessibility tree (a menu is a list of items with prices); questions never rely on colour alone.
- Offline: all lesson content is in the bundle; no network call.
- Privacy: no lesson collects, stores or sends free text, a photo, a voice or the learner's name. The one exception to "no free text" is Write Your Name q3, where the learner composes their name on screen: it lives in component state only, is excluded from telemetry, breadcrumbs, logs and persisted state, and is cleared on unmount (section 3.7, OD-6, test P-1); progress is the existing quest completion only.
- Layout: 320 px phone to tablet landscape (F-LAYOUT-001); Korean lines wrap at spaces (`word-break: keep-all` on web).

### 3.15 Behaviours (Given / When / Then)

1. **Given** a cell file with both MAR sign-offs, **when** `build-lessons.mjs` runs, **then** the cell's episode replaces its placeholder in `episodesAll`, the grid opens it, and the catalogue cell is `shipped` with the same minutes and quest ids (parity).
2. **Given** a cell file with `review.culture: "draft"`, **when** the build runs, **then** the cell is left out and still reads "Coming later"; CI is green.
3. **Given** a Stage 3 quest, **when** the learner taps a wrong particle tile, **then** the amber nudge and Hoya's `thinking` pose show, the tile slides back, the round is scored on the first answer only, and no meter drops.
4. **Given** a Stage 7 Write Frame, **when** the learner picks any word from the bank, **then** the pick is accepted; **when** they type it with one syllable different, **then** the nudge shows the picked word under the typed one and they can fix it; nothing typed is stored after the quest.
5. **Given** the free-plan switch on and a free learner, **when** they open Holiday Words, **then** they see "Not open yet" and the preview; **given** the switch off, **then** the quest opens.
6. **Given** a learner placed at `stage3` band 0 (including one whose result was stored at catalogue version 8, before any Stage 2 cell shipped), **when** the Journey opens, **then** the next quest is the first Stage 3 quest still to play, and every Stage 2 quest stays playable.
7. **Given** no Korean voice, **when** a listen round of a Stage 4 dialogue starts, **then** the line shows as text with its romanization and gloss, and the round can be answered.
8. **Given** a teacher's weekly plan made at catalogue version 8 that names `episode:stage2-rites`, **when** Holiday Words ships, **then** the plan's item expands to its three quests at derivation, and the wizard offers Refresh route. An installed native app older than the content counts the new quest ids as not ready (F-PLAN-002 Q4) and keeps showing the placeholder until the app updates; the web console and PWA get the new catalogue when they deploy.
9. **Given** any lesson screen, **when** it is read in the Korean UI, **then** menus are Korean and taught items still carry romanization and a gloss, and the Korean gloss never repeats the Korean it explains.
10. **Given** Write Your Name q3, **when** the learner composes their name and finishes the quest, **then** no emitted telemetry event, breadcrumb, log line or storage key contains the composed string, and after the Write Frame step (with its "My page") unmounts the string is gone.

## 9. Unverified assumptions

1. **Bundle estimate.** 6-7 KB raw / 1.3-1.6 KB gzip per upper-stage quest is extrapolated from the shipped 18 quests; S2-1 measures the first real cell and records it here. The budget of section 3.12 is the guard.
2. **Lazy `require` under Metro web.** Metro web bundles the per-stage modules into the one bundle; the lazy `require` defers evaluation, not download. C-2 measures start-up evaluation with and without the modules (V8 proxy, as F-STORY-001 PR 1.9 did).
3. **Trace a Word stroke data.** The jamo stroke skeletons (`content/jamo-strokes.ts`) are drawn for a jamo alone; their placement inside a syllable block (narrow initial, vowel at right or below, final at the bottom) needs a position transform. G-4 prototypes it on the 50 syllables of S7-1 first; if shapes read poorly at block size, Trace a Word traces the word's letters one at a time in block position.
4. **WordArt coverage.** Most Stage 2-3 nouns have a `word:` picture (food, animals, nature, school, sports, k-culture, family topics); a few (복주머니, 세뱃돈, 미끄럼틀, 그네, 모래, 숨바꼭질) may not. Those are taught as reading words only, or a cell row adds the glyph through F-VOC-002's art budget (its 160 KB / 48 KB ceiling has about 21 KB raw left).
5. **Sound contrasts.** The ㅐ / ㅔ and ㅚ / ㅙ / ㅞ notes describe common present-day pronunciation; the language review confirms the wording against the standard pronunciation rules and its sources before S2-1 ships.
